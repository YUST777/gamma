The 2026/2027 Road to ICPC: Season Pathway

A 7-phase algorithmic curriculum guiding students from basic programming fundamentals to national ECPC selection.

Phase & Stage Timeline Core Technical Focus & Milestone Events
Phase 1: Recruitment & Orientation Aug 29 – Oct 9, 2026 Campus promotion, online forms, interviews, Orientation Day, Intro Session.
Phase 2: Programming Foundations Oct 16 – Nov 26, 2026 Variables, loops, arrays, strings, functions, Big-O complexity; weekly practice contests.
Phase 3: STL & Winter Checkpoint Nov 27, 2026 – Jan 29, 2027 Vectors, stacks, queues, sets, maps, priority queues; Warm-up Contest: Jan 29, 2027
Phase 4: Core Problem-Solving Feb 5 – Mar 19, 2027 Frequency arrays, two pointers, binary search, greedy algorithms, recursion, backtracking.
Phase 5: Advanced Algorithms Mar 26 – Apr 30, 2027 Mathematics I & II, Dynamic Programming (DP I & II), Graph Algorithms I.
Phase 6: Team Formation & Selection Apr 24 – Jun 18, 2027 Internal team trials, Team Selection Contest (TSC), official team roster announcement.
Phase 7: Final ECPC Preparation Jun 22 – Aug 15, 2027 Intensive weekly 5-hour simulated contests, full tournament simulation, target ECPC 2027.
* Unless otherwise stated, technical sessions are held Fridays at 8:30 PM. Dates marked as estimated adjust in accordance with the official Horus University academic calendar.
