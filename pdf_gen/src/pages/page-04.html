Tournament Profile: What is DCC? Inaugural Damietta Edition

The Damietta Collegiate Contest (DCC) is the premier regional competitive programming championship in Damietta governorate. Co-founded and co-organized in direct partnership between ACPC Club Damietta University and ICPC HUE (Horus University), DCC was engineered to unite collegiate coders across five Delta university institutions. Operating as an official pre-ECPC tournament proving ground, DCC simulated full international collegiate contest conditions—featuring 2 phases (online preliminary & onsite timed lab final), live judging, and standardized algorithmic problem sets.
