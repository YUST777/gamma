The ICPC HUE Platform: Closed-Loop Tracking

A custom-engineered training ecosystem uniting daily student habit logging, real-time mentor telemetry, and deep code inspection into an automated, verifiable system.

Pillar 02 · Real-Time Mentor Dashboard & Consistency Heatmap LIVE TELEMETRY
PORTAL: ICPCHUE.COM

Automated Performance Telemetry: Mentors monitor active trainee volume in real time (e.g. 185 solved problems, 29% curriculum completion, 371 submissions), verify daily practice consistency via a 365-day activity heatmap, and detect participation dips before students fall behind.

Pillar 01 · Self-Discipline & Study Log
HABIT ENGINE

Structured Habit Formation: Students record daily practice hours, problem sets completed, study references, and self-reflections. Mentors review weekly logs and provide direct feedback, instilling accountability and steady momentum.

Pillar 03 · Problem Code Inspector
CODE-LEVEL AUDIT

Targeted Technical Interventions: Instructors and mentors review students' exact C++ implementations, attempt logs (AC, WA, TLE), and STL usage—tailoring weekly review sessions based on actual aggregate failure rates across problem sheets.

Institutional Result: Seamless transition from informal, unmonitored follow-up to a quantitative, verifiable collegiate training system.
