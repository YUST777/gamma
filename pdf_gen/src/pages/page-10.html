Building Our Committees: A Two-Phase Growth Story

How ICPC HUE established its four specialized committees: starting with the technical core of instructors and mentors, then launching the public "Hire Up" campaign at icpchue.com/job.

The Strategic Objective: Building a Sustainable Operating Model 2-STAGE RECRUITMENT

To transition ICPC HUE from a student-led initiative into an enduring institutional power at Horus University, our top priority was assembling specialized community committees. Rather than an uncoordinated mass callout, leadership executed a deliberate, two-phase strategy: first securing our technical training foundation, and then expanding campus-wide to recruit creative and operational leaders.

PHASE 01 The Technical Foundation
INSTRUCTORS & MENTORS

We recognized that before promoting events or expanding membership, we needed an elite technical engine. We turned directly to the arena-proven competitors who led Horus University at the ECPC qualifications:

Top-5 Team Interviews

Conducted in-depth interviews with candidates from the top five teams (four teams from AI, one from Engineering). Evaluated algorithmic depth (STL, recursion, problem-solving), pedagogical clarity, and peer empathy.

Interviews and technical vetting were supervised by faculty engineers: Eng. Fatma Magdy, Eng. Sara Lotfy, and Eng. Raafat Mohamed.

Milestone 1 Completed:
Secured Lead & Co-Lead Instructors and dedicated Mentors for all training cohorts.
PHASE 02 Campus-Wide Expansion
HIRE UP & /JOB

With the technical core firmly established, the community needed creative storytellers, video editors, designers, and operational coordinators to manage large-scale campus logistics and freshman outreach:

"Hire Up" Campaign & Portal icpchue.com/job

Published the public "Hire Up" announcement across Horus University and launched a dedicated digital application system at icpchue.com/job to recruit the remaining committees:

Media Committee Operations Committee (OC)

Candidates applied with portfolios in Premiere Pro, After Effects, and event management—vetting each applicant for commitment and collegiate initiative.

Milestone 2 Completed:
Successfully recruited the full Media production squad and the 11-member Organizing Committee.

By securing our technical instruction and mentoring engine first before expanding into media and event operations, ICPC HUE ensured that every marketing campaign and campus event is anchored by genuine educational substance and verifiable student outcomes.
