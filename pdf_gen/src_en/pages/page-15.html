The ICPC Path 2026/2027: Season Phases

A 7-phase curriculum taking students from programming basics to team selection and competing in ECPC.

PhasePeriodTechnical Content and Key Milestones
Phase 1: Outreach & Orientation29 Aug – 9 Oct 2026University marketing campaign, application form, interviews, orientation day, and opening session.
Phase 2: Programming Basics16 Oct – 26 Nov 2026Variables, loops, arrays, strings, functions, time complexity (Big-O), and weekly practice contests.
Phase 3: STL & Winter Checkpoint27 Nov 2026 – 29 Jan 2027vector, stack, queue, set, map, priority_queue; Warm-up Contest: 29 Jan 2027
Phase 4: Problem-Solving Techniques5 Feb – 19 Mar 2027Frequency arrays, two pointers, binary search, greedy algorithms, recursion, and backtracking.
Phase 5: Advanced Algorithms26 Mar – 30 Apr 2027Math 1 & 2, Dynamic Programming 1 & 2, and Graph Algorithms 1.
Phase 6: Team Formation & Selection24 Apr – 18 Jun 2027Internal team trials, the official Team Selection Contest (TSC), and announcement of approved teams.
Phase 7: Final ECPC Preparation22 Jun – 15 Aug 2027Intensive weekly 5-hour contests, a full contest simulation, and the goal: ECPC 2027.

* Unless stated otherwise, technical sessions are held on Fridays at 8:30 PM. Dates are estimates and may shift with Horus University's official academic calendar.
