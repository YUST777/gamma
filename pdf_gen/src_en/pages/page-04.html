What we achieved last season: the beginning

Key milestones in order, from our first announcement in November 2025 to Orientation Day in March 2026.

+500students enrolled in trainingduring the season
21students completed trainingby season end, in 7 teams
16teams represented Horusat the ECPC 2026 qualifiers
+22hours of recorded sessionsavailable on the platform
  • 26 – 28 Nov 2025
    Community launch and registration: the first competitive programming community in the Faculty of AI. Over 300 students registered in the first days, and the platform launched on 28 Nov.
  • 7 Dec
    Live opening session with over 160 students; we closed the form after requests passed 250.
  • 8 – 12 Dec
    Admission camp: kickoff session (8 Dec on Telegram), Conditions and Control Flow (9), IF / ELSE & SWITCH (10), review and problem solving (11), and the admission exam (12 Dec).
  • 14 Dec
    Platform walkthrough video after the latest update, showing students how to use it.
  • 20 – 26 Dec
    Winter camp: session on 20 Dec, first sheet and a submission guide video (24 Dec), the leaderboard went live, and recordings of all four sessions uploaded to the platform (26 Dec).
  • 1 – 2 Jan 2026
    Programming 1 camp: a full course review and a live problem-solving stream, with the recording uploaded before the faculty exam on 3 Jan.
  • 3 Jan
    Official launch of the learning platform with an intro video: instant judge, side-by-side input and output, session library and leaderboards.
  • 26 Jan
    Team returned after exams and announced the winter break camp.
  • February 2026
    Team officially registered with Student Affairs under the supervision of Dr. Basma Mostafa and Dr. Yasser El-Awady. The 650-problem curriculum was completed, the Verdict Helper extension launched (9 Feb), and we registered icpchue.com (25 Feb).
  • Until 6 Mar
    17 hours of live streams in Level 0: data types, loops, arrays and functions.
  • 12 March 2026
    Orientation Day in Hall H403, Faculty of AI, our first official offline event (details on page 7).
