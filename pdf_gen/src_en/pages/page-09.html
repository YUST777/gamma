Horus University's first participation

16 teams at the ECPC 2026 qualifiers in Alexandria

For the first time, 16 teams represented Horus University at the ECPC qualifiers at the Arab Academy for Science, Technology and Maritime Transport, including the teams ICPC HUE trained all season.
