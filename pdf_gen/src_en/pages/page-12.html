Keeping students going

Leaderboard and achievements: training as a game

Problem: The hardest part of competitive programming is consistency. Over 500 students registered with us, only 21 made it to the qualifiers, and most who stopped lost motivation in the first weeks. Solution: A full motivation layer: rankings that update with every problem, badges to earn, and real-world prizes.

Leaderboard: students ranked by accepted solves in training sheets, with a second tab for Codeforces rating (names hidden).
Achievements: welcome badge, Level 0 completed, first sheet solved, and +500 rating.
Daily streak and consistency heatmap on the student dashboard.
Students don't know how they rank among peers
Two leaderboards: platform sheets and global Codeforces rating. Since 24 Dec 2025 we post the weekly leaders.
Competition without prizes fades fast
Since 15 Mar 2026, first place wins an ICPC HUE T-shirt. Mostafa Kamal, March's champion, won it (3 Apr).
Digital achievements go unseen
Each achievement unlocked earns a laptop sticker; the first batch was handed out on Orientation Day, 12 Mar.
Dropping off starts with one day
A streak counter and consistency heatmap reveal any gap, so students return before falling behind.
