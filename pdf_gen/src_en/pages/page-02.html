About the Community

Who we are, and why this work deserves support

ICPC HUE is a student community founded in the Faculty of AI that serves all faculties of Horus University. We train students over a full season (one academic year) to compete in the ICPC.

The idea is not new: after ECPC in Egypt asked every university to form a student team to train its students, almost every participating university now has a community like ours. We are Horus University's community, active since November 2025, and we want Horus to hold a permanent place among these universities.

What is the ICPC?

The largest and oldest university programming contest in the world. Students compete in teams of 3 from the same university, sharing one computer, to solve a set of programming problems in 5 hours. The team that solves the most problems in the least time wins. Teams advance through these stages:

University QualifiersTeams from the same university compete first
→
ECPCThe Egyptian contest, held each August
→

What do we study? Problem Solving

We take a logic problem and turn it into a correct, fast program. A simple example: you have 4 sticks of different lengths. Can you form a square with them? Given the lengths 8 6 11 12, the answer is no, because the sides are unequal. Given 8 8 8 8, the answer is yes.

As students progress, problems get harder and require algorithms and data structures such as STL, binary search, dynamic programming and graphs, and the solution must run in a fraction of a second on huge inputs.

// Input: 8 8 8 8
read a, b, c, d
print(a == b && b == c && c == d)
// Output: true

Why does it matter?

For the student
  • A filter for employers: the contest shows who truly codes well and understands hard topics.
  • A path to a job: interviews at companies such as Noon and the global FAANG companies are built on Problem Solving questions, which are hard to pass without training.
  • A stronger CV: competing and working in committees is real experience for the CV.
For the university
  • Horus in the results: the university's name appears in the contest rankings alongside every university in Egypt.
  • In-demand graduates: students with a strong foundation in programming and logical thinking, which the job market seeks.
  • Sustainable activity: today's contestants become tomorrow's instructors, passing experience from one cohort to the next.
