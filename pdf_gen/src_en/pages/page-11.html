10 · Platform (2/4): Curriculum and Sessions
A clear path from the first line of code

650 ordered problems, and every session in one place

Problem: New students don't know where to start or what to solve after each session. Online problems are many and unordered, so they quit within weeks. Solution: A curriculum split into levels, each level into sheets, each sheet tied to a session, plus a roadmap showing where the student stands at any moment.

The three levels: Beginners, Level 1, and advanced Level 2.
"Data Types and Conditions" sheet: 26 problems and the student's progress bar.
LevelProblemsTopics (roadmap prepared with the Assiut ICPC community)
Level 0: Beginners249 in 10 sheetsData types and conditions, loops, arrays, strings, functions, math and geometry, recursion, and general problems at three difficulty tiers.
Level 1: Intermediate226 in 8 sheetsTime complexity, STL containers and sorting, binary search and two pointers, sliding window, Bitmask, number theory, and prefix sums.
Level 2: Advanced171 problemsAdvanced contest-level data structures and algorithms.
Roadmap: the student's progress in each topic.
STL 1 session: the video inside the platform with a topic description.
Missed sessions used to be lost
A student who missed a session waited a week for it to be posted in a group. Now 12 recorded sessions across 3 camps live on the platform, each with its sheet on the same day.
Videos were heavy on weak connections
In September 2026 we moved all sessions to YouTube (unlisted), played from inside the platform, so they load fast on any connection.
