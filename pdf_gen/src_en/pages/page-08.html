DCC 2026: the real start of competitive programming in Damietta

DCC (Damietta Competitive Contest) is the first competitive programming contest in the Damietta region. It was fully designed and run by ACPC Club Damietta University (ACPC DU) and hosted by Horus University at the Faculty of AI; our community helped with media, contest-day management and the website. The goal: a first real step for competitive programming in the region, and preparing students for ECPC.

Over 100 contestants from 5 communities at the DCC final, hosted by the Faculty of AI at Horus University.
