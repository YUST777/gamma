March – October 2026

What we achieved last season: from training to competition

From team formation to the ECPC qualifiers, then preparing for the new season.

  • 14 – 15 Mar
    One-step registration at icpchue.com/register replacing the form, and the start of the T-shirt challenge for the leaderboard's top spot.
  • 2 – 4 Apr
    Explaining the ECPC: its stages, rules and fees; honoring Mostafa Kamal as March champion (3 Apr); and a live Q&A stream (4 Apr).
  • 7 Apr
    First ICPC HUE contest for team formation in Lab E425, Faculty of Engineering: Ibrahim Matar, then Ahmed Waleed, then Noha Kamal.
  • 10 Apr
    Live Functions session, and the Functions and Recursion sessions uploaded to the platform.
  • 14 – 30 Apr
    Second team-formation contest in Hall A209 (14 Apr); ECPC registration closed on 30 Apr with 7 teams: 4 from Level 1 and 3 from Level 2.
  • May 2026
    Beginner track sessions completed: data and conditions, loops, arrays, strings, functions and recursion.
  • 12 May
    ECPC local qualifiers at the faculty under the patronage of Prof. Dr. Ibrahim Saber, Chairman of the University Board of Trustees, with 6 teams.
  • Summer 2026
    Level 1 training with Time Complexity and STL 1 sessions on the platform, and a month of intensive review of advanced topics before the qualifiers.
  • 26 Jul – 9 Aug
    Hosting the DCC contest organized by ACPC DU: registration until 29 Jul, online round 31 Jul, final at the faculty 6 Aug, winners announced 9 Aug.
  • 17 – 25 Aug
    ECPC qualifiers: our teams announced (17), travel to Alexandria (18), 16 teams represented Horus for the first time (24), and the qualifiers trip post (25).
  • 23 – 27 Aug
    Arabic FAQ page about the ICPC on the platform, and the volunteer portal icpchue.com/job.
  • September 2026
    New season preparation: interviews with the top 5 teams to select instructors and mentors, the mentor dashboard (2 Sep), the daily study log (8 Sep), and moving sessions to YouTube.
  • 23 – 24 Sep
    Honoring the 2025/2026 Board, and the “Hire Up” campaign to join the 2027 season team, which received 50 applicants.
  • 2 October 2026
    Level 0 registration opened for the new season on the platform.
