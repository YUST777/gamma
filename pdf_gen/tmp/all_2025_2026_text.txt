ICPC HUE Community Progress Report
JANUARY – MAY 2026
From a bold winter launch to seven fully-formed competitive teams, the ICPC HUE community has
built something extraordinary in just five months. This report captures the complete journey —
platform launches, elite mentorship events, intensive training, official competitions, and the
exceptional numbers that prove a new era of competitive programming has arrived at Horus
University.

January marked the cornerstone of everything that followed. The ICPC HUE community announced
a strong, determined return for the new season with the launch of the Winter Camp — a structured
program designed to develop students' problem-solving skills, prepare them for upcoming
competitions, and connect those skills to real-world opportunities at major tech companies like
Noon. This was not just a warm-up; it was a declaration of intent.
The Winter Camp served a dual purpose: reigniting the competitive spirit of returning members
while opening the door wide for newcomers. Registration was opened specifically to target new
students who wanted to start their competitive programming journey from scratch, while
simultaneously confirming the spots of previously registered members in preparation for the camp's
intensive schedule. The result was a growing, energized community united by a shared goal.
The January foundation was critical because it set the tone for everything that followed. By
welcoming both experienced competitors and absolute beginners under the same banner, ICPC HUE
established itself as an inclusive yet ambitious community. The Winter Camp wasn't just about
solving problems — it was about building identity, momentum, and a shared sense of purpose that
would carry the community through the intense months ahead.

April was the month of decision. All the preparation, training, and platform building culminated in one
clear objective: forming the strongest possible teams to represent Horus University in the ECPC
(Egyptian Collegiate Programming Contest). The community shifted into high gear, with every
session, contest, and announcement focused on this singular goal.
The official ECPC announcement outlined the full competition roadmap, giving students a clear
vision of the journey ahead: from University Qualifiers to ECPC, then ACPC, and ultimately the ICPC
World Finals. An intensive 3-month training period was announced to prepare teams for each
stage. ECPC registration officially closed on April 30, marking the end of the enrollment phase and
the beginning of the final sprint.
To support students through this critical period, the community hosted a dedicated live stream to
answer all questions regarding ECPC rules, team formation requirements, and training expectations.
Additionally, the Functions and Recursion sessions were successfully uploaded to the platform's
dashboard, ensuring that students had on-demand access to key educational content even outside
of live sessions. These resources were essential for students who needed to balance competition
preparation with their academic schedules.
