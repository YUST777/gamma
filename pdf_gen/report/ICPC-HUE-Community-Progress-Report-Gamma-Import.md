# Card 07: The Four Community Committees

**Kicker:** 06 / OPERATING MODEL  
## The Four Community Committees: Structured Specialization
*A defined functional division of labor designed to overcome technical gaps, inconsistent follow-up, and logistical overhead.*

> **Foundational Leadership Principle:** No member is forced into a particular committee. Each student actively selects the role matching their skills and passion, ensuring high intrinsic accountability and pedagogical integrity.

### 01 · Instructors Committee
Deliver weekly technical lectures, solve problems live with step-by-step clarity, and prepare structured problem sheets. Instructors must demonstrate academic honesty, deep algorithmic mastery, and the patience required to guide novice programmers.

### 02 · Mentors Committee
Track small teams continuously, conduct weekly check-ins, identify when students encounter conceptual roadblocks, and monitor student wellbeing. Mentors ensure students maintain consistent practice habits rather than dropping off.

### 03 · Media Committee
Produce high-quality presentations, promotional videos (using Adobe Premiere and After Effects), and social media documentation. They celebrate student achievements, elevate community visibility, and drive recruitment.

### 04 · Operations Committee
Manage physical logistics, coordinate lab reservations with university administration, organize on-campus event flows, oversee printing of materials, and ensure smooth operational execution during all offline contests.

# Card 09: The ICPC HUE Platform: Closed-Loop Tracking

**Kicker:** 08 / DIGITAL PLATFORM  
## The ICPC HUE Platform: Closed-Loop Tracking
*A custom-engineered training ecosystem uniting daily student habit logging, real-time mentor telemetry, and deep code inspection into an automated, verifiable system.*

### Pillar 01 · Self-Discipline & Daily Study Log (Habit Engine)
- **Image:** `report/assets/platform/study_discipline_log.png`
- **Features:** Students record daily study hours, problem sets completed, study references, and self-reflections. Mentors review weekly logs and provide direct feedback, instilling personal accountability and steady momentum.

### Pillar 02 · Real-Time Mentor Dashboard & 365-Day Consistency Heatmap (Hero Feature)
- **Image:** `report/assets/platform/mentor_dashboard.png`
- **Portal:** `icpchue.com` (120+ Active Trainees)
- **Features:** Mentors monitor active trainee volume in real time (e.g. 185 solved problems, 29% curriculum completion, 371 submissions), verify daily practice consistency via a GitHub-style 365-day activity heatmap, and detect participation dips before students fall behind.

### Pillar 03 · Problem Code Inspector & Technical Intervention (Code-Level Audit)
- **Image:** `report/assets/platform/code_inspector.png`
- **Features:** Instructors and mentors review students' exact C++ implementations, attempt logs (AC, WA, TLE), and STL usage—tailoring weekly review sessions based on actual aggregate failure rates across problem sheets.

> **Institutional Result:** Seamless transition from informal, unmonitored follow-up to a quantitative, verifiable collegiate training system.
